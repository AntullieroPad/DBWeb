Meet the Board

Here at DB Yale, we’re more than just colleagues—we’re Bori-buddies! Get to know the team behind our activism, creative projects, and community outreach.

Pin Your Eyes on Our Bori‑Buddies!

Nina Feliciano-Bautista

Co-President

Nina co-leads DB's direction and turns it into something that runs: the timelines, the partnerships, the details that carry an idea from a board meeting to a room full of people. An organizer in her own right, she has worked with Yale's Latino archives to keep our community's record in our own hands.

nina.feliciano-bautista@yale.edu

Sonia Rosa

Co-President

Sonia co-leads DB's direction and keeps asking the long question: what this community should look like in ten years, and what we owe the Bori-buddies who come after us. An activist in her own right, she has worked with Yale's Latino archives to bring Puerto Rican histories into the light.

sonia.rosa@yale.edu

Antonio Padilla

Coalition Chair

Antonio leads DB's political initiatives and builds our coalitions with organizations across Yale and beyond, bridging activism and academia and uplifting new voices.

antonio.padilla@yale.edu

Daniel Torres

Co-Treasurer

Daniel manages DB's financial health, ensuring every project is feasible and every event stays on budget.

daniel.torres@yale.edu

Natalia Andreu-Bird

Co-Treasurer

Natalia shares treasurer duties, tracking DB's budget and keeping our events and initiatives funded throughout the year.

natalia.andreu-bird@yale.edu

Patricia Huerta de Mend

Social Chair

Patricia plans DB's socials, from cooking nights to cultural showcases, making sure every gathering feels like familia.

patricia.huerta@yale.edu

Kelly Chamber

Marketing & Communications Chair

Kelly runs DB's marketing and communications, keeping our social media, newsletters, and outreach loud and clear.

kelly@yale.edu

Jay Hatch

First Year Peer Liaison

Jay welcomes incoming Yalies into the DB community and is the first point of contact for first-years finding their footing.

jay.hatch@yale.edu

Ana Cristina Betancourt

Graduate Peer Liaison

Ana Cristina bridges the graduate and undergraduate communities, connecting and amplifying Puerto Rican voices across Yale.

ana.betancourt@yale.edu
