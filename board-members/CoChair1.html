Nina Feliciano-Bautista

Co-President

From San Juan, Puerto Rico, with family in San Juan and Bayamón.

As Co-President, Nina co-leads DB's direction alongside Sonia and turns it into something that runs — the timelines, the partnerships, and the details that carry an idea from a board meeting to a room full of people. She previously served as DB's Cafecito Coordinator, anchoring one of our signature traditions: a warm, consistent weekly space for Boricuas to gather, recharge, and build community across the semester.

An organizer in her own right, she has worked with Yale's Latino archives to keep our community's record in our own hands.

And, as a fun fact, Nina once met Zendaya at age ten, which she mentions only if prompted.
