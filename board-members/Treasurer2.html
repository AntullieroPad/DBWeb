Natalia Andreu-Bird

Co-Treasurer

Natalia comes from the lovely city of Guaynabo, Puerto Rico. She is a sophomore at Yale College studying Mathematics & Philosophy.

Growing up in Puerto Rico, she’s seen firsthand the importance of pride in culture and how necessary the unity of a community is. As DB’s Co-Treasurer, she wants to help facilitate the financial side of planning events and make sure every project is funded and on budget.

In her spare time, she loves spending time with friends, going out, photography, watching movies and shows, talking about history and politics, reading, and listening to music.

Fun fact: Natalia is a black belt in Taekwondo — though she earned it pre-COVID, so she’ll be the first to tell you she’s incredibly rusty.
