Antonio Padilla

Coalition Chair

Antonio Padilla is a junior at Hopper College who comes from Chicago to two parents born and raised in Puerto Rico — his father from Manatí and his mother from Mayagüez. This strong connection to the island informs his dedication to the Boricua community at Yale.

As Coalition Chair, Antonio leads DB's political initiatives and builds our coalitions with organizations across Yale and beyond, bridging activism and academia and uplifting new voices.

An active student athlete, Antonio can often be found at the PWG (Payne Whitney Gymnasium) working out, practicing with the wrestling team, or training in Taekwondo.

Antonio describes himself as "a hip-hop, rock head exploring a wide range of music," with recent interests spanning from Radiohead and Red Hot Chili Peppers, to Brockhampton and Clipse, to Marvin Gaye. His personal favorite artists are Kid Cudi and Kendrick Lamar.

His summer reading list reveals his literary interests: "White Nights + Notes From Underground, Part Time Indian, Maus, The Alchemist, Heart of Darkness, The Pearl, Tuesdays With Morrie, The Metamorphosis, and The Crucible (currently)."

Perhaps most intriguing is Antonio's collection of unusual hobbies: "I'm a hobbyist who picks up random stuff: self-taught oboe-ocarina-harp playing, book and word collecting, slacklining, and lockpicking (successfully)." He jokingly advises, "Keep your bookshelves locked up around me."

Beyond his official role, Antonio is known to "chef it up for any and everyone who is hungry," combining his culinary skills with his natural hospitality to nurture the DB Yale community.
