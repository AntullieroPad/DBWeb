Ana Cristina Betancourt

Graduate Peer Liaison

Ana Cristina is from Caguas, Puerto Rico, and a proud graduate of the University of Puerto Rico.

She’s pursuing a PhD in the History of Science at Yale.

As Graduate Peer Liaison, Ana Cristina is the bridge between Puerto Rican graduate students and the undergraduate community. She connects Bori-buddies across schools and departments, builds mentorship where it didn’t exist before, and makes sure graduate voices are part of everything DB does.

When she’s not connecting the two halves of Boricua Yale, she is making what she will confidently tell you is the best flan you have ever tasted 🍮 — and honestly, we don’t argue.
